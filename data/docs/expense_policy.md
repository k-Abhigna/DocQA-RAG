# Nimbus Labs Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting Expenses
Expenses must be submitted in the Expensify app within 30 days of purchase,
with an itemised receipt attached. Claims submitted after 30 days need written
approval from the Finance Director.

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed
for flights of 6 hours or longer. Hotel bookings are capped at 150 USD per night
in standard cities and 250 USD per night in high-cost cities such as New York,
London, and Tokyo.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance while travelling is 60 USD. Alcohol is not
reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client dinner.

## Home Office
Each employee may claim a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD for a desk,
chair, or monitor. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.
