# Nimbus Labs IT Security Guidelines

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
is mandatory for email, the VPN, and GitHub. Use the company password manager,
1Password, to store all work credentials.

## Devices
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock
automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system updates must be
installed within 14 days of release.

## Reporting Incidents
Report a lost device or suspected phishing email to security@nimbuslabs.example
within 1 hour. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues.

## Data Handling
Customer data must never be stored on personal devices or uploaded to personal
cloud storage. Confidential documents are shared only through the company
Google Drive with link sharing restricted to the organisation.
