# Nimbus Labs Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 5 unused days can be carried
over into the next year; any balance above 5 days expires on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not
carry over to the next year.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave, which must be taken within
6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Requesting Leave
All leave is requested through the HR portal at least 7 days in advance, except
for sick leave. Your manager must approve the request within 3 working days.
